Who we are

Environmental & infrastructure management, built on field experience.

M/s. Planet Protectors Enviro Private Limited (PPEPL) was incorporated in May 2025 to provide comprehensive environmental and infrastructure management solutions — particularly focused on bio-mining of legacy waste, pyrolysis projects, electrical project execution, and infrastructure operations management.

Our leadership team brings over two decades of managerial experience and more than seven years of specialized experience in bio-mining, waste handling, and environmental operations — making us a trusted partner to Government bodies, Municipal Corporations, and Environmental Agencies across India.

  • Scientific, bio-mining–led approach to legacy waste reclamation
  • Integrated electrical, civil, and project management capability
  • Direct coordination with municipal officials and pollution control boards
  • Registered office in Madhavaram, Tiruvallur District, Tamil Nadu
What we deliver

Ten integrated service lines, one accountable team.

From reclaiming legacy landfills to keeping electrical systems running, our services cover the full lifecycle of environmental and infrastructure projects.

01

Bio-Mining & Waste Management

Scientific reclamation of legacy waste dumps using approved bio-mining methods — excavators, trommels, conveyors, and screeners — with sorting, transportation, remediation, and daily progress reporting.

02

Electrical Projects & Installations

All kinds of electrical and automation works — transformers, SSB panels, factory and house wiring, CEIG certificate approval, earthing, lighting, and control systems — plus preventive maintenance.

03

Battery-Operated Vehicle (BOV) Supply & Operation

Supply and complete O&M of battery-operated waste collection fleets — route planning, manpower deployment, tracking, and reporting for smart city and municipal collection.

04

Incinerator — AMC & O&M

Annual Maintenance Contracts, daily operation and supervision, skilled operator supply, emission control, and safety compliance, with full logbook maintenance.

05

Plastic Pyrolysis Plant — O&M

Supply, installation, and O&M of pyrolysis plants — feedstock and process control, oil/gas/char handling, plant optimization, and preventive maintenance for maximum fuel output.

06

RDF & Biomass Material Transportation

Supply and transportation of RDF to cement and paper mills, biomass for industrial boilers — own fleet of tippers, trucks and loaders, with GPS tracking and weighbridge coordination.

07

Project Management & Cost Control

End-to-end planning, scheduling, and budgeting; Work Breakdown Structures and progress tracking; strict safety and statutory compliance; regular financial monitoring.

08

Manpower & Machinery Supply

Skilled and semi-skilled labourers, engineers, supervisors, and operators; heavy earthmoving machinery, screening units, and conveyors, with on-site installation and support.

09

Civil Projects & Interiors

Civil construction for Government and private projects — commercial, industrial, and residential structures — plus renovation, restoration, and complete interior execution.

10

Site Coordination & Documentation

Coordination with municipal officials, pollution control boards, and client representatives; preparation and submission of project documentation, reports, and compliance forms.
